FAQ

The things clients ask first.

How much do acoustic treatments cost?

From ₹150/sq ft for basic treatments up to ₹400–800/sq ft for premium installations, depending on size, material and performance target. We quote after a free site assessment.

What’s the difference between soundproofing and acoustic treatment?

Soundproofing blocks sound between spaces; acoustic treatment controls reflections within one. Most projects need both — we work out the right mix for yours.

How long does acoustic installation take?

1–2 days for a small room, 3–5 days for a medium space, 1–2 weeks for a large facility (500+ sq m). Bigger or more complex projects run to a custom timeline.

Do you provide warranties on acoustic products?

Yes — 5–10 years on materials, 2 years on installation, plus a performance guarantee against your contracted targets. Annual maintenance packages are available.

Can acoustic treatments be customized for interior design?

Yes. Our fabric-wrapped panels and acoustic tiles come in hundreds of colours, patterns and textures, and we work directly with interior designers on the look.
