Type and palette

The three families

One role each. Hierarchy in the display face is width, not weight — everything is held at --w-display 600.

Aa Spline Sans Mono

Every label, spec figure, button and filter pill.

--mono: 'Spline Sans Mono', ui-monospace, 'SF Mono', Menlo, monospace

The NRC bar

The one signature device. NRC is already a proportion — 0.85 means 85% of incident sound energy absorbed — so the bar states the figure rather than illustrating it. Filled run is the absorbed share in the material’s own colour; the empty run is the paper, i.e. what comes back off the wall. It renders only where the client publishes an NRC: 13 of 19 products. The rest show nothing rather than a bar at an invented value.

Acoustic Polyester Panel

0.85 NRCup to 0.6 (9 mm) / up to 0.85 (12 mm)

Acoustic Wedge Foam

0.90 NRCup to 0.9

Acoustic Wooden Slats

0.85 NRCup to 0.85

The banner is measured separately

Its type sits on a swappable photograph behind a white two-gradient scrim, so no token pair states it. The scrim floor across the left half, where the type sits, is 0.643 — over the worst case a photograph can present (pure black) that is 7.1:1. Measured against the real composite instead of the worst case, by replicating the scrim on a canvas over the actual image and sampling inside the eyebrow, h1 and lead boxes: 8.99:1 for --on-banner and 4.75:1 for --mark-banner. Lower those alphas to show more of the picture and the arithmetic has to be redone against black, not against the photograph you like.
